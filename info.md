Luba Scheduler schedules and supervises a Mammotion Luba robot mower: it decides when conditions allow a mow, asks you before starting, verifies the job really started, and stops the mower when the weather turns.

**Beta, shadow mode by default.** The engine runs the whole mowing day and logs every decision, but in shadow mode it never commands the mower, notifies or writes the calendar until you switch it to active in Options → Mode.
